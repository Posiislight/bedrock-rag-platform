Bedrock LLM + RAG platform

How a request moves through the system

A FastAPI service on EC2 calls Amazon Bedrock for chat and embeddings, retrieves from S3-backed documents, runs slow work through SQS, and reports every call to CloudWatch. All five stacks are CloudFormation.
